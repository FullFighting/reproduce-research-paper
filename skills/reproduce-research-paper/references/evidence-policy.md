# Evidence and status policy

## Required chain

A positive claim needs all of the following:

1. a precise claim and paper location;
2. whole-paper context and the paper's declared protocol;
3. exact repository/environment state;
4. authorized commands with successful run records;
5. no original-source mutation or undeclared write/capability violation;
6. a log or result file generated by that run;
7. a predeclared machine extractor and source hash;
8. a normalized observation with units;
9. a predeclared comparison that passes;
10. a valid evidence-bundle verification.

Missing any item prevents `CLAIM_REPRODUCED_WITHIN_TOLERANCE`.

## Status meanings

- `CLAIM_REPRODUCED_WITHIN_TOLERANCE`: the declared computational claim passed the recorded protocol and comparison. It does not verify the whole paper.
- `CLAIM_NOT_REPRODUCED`: a valid executed and comparable observation fell outside policy.
- `PARTIALLY_REPRODUCED`: at least one declared claim passed and another did not pass, was blocked, or was inconclusive.
- `INCONCLUSIVE`: evidence was invalidated, underspecified, statistically insufficient, or otherwise not interpretable.
- `BLOCKED`: a required command, artifact, dependency, license, access path, or extractor was unavailable.
- `NOT_ATTEMPTED`: no relevant execution occurred.
- `MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`: the target requires human reading of a table, figure, image, or other non-machine observation.

Do not use `VERIFIED`, `CONTRADICTED`, or `NOT_RUN` in V1 reports.

## Statistical policy

- Predeclare absolute, relative, and rounding tolerances.
- Set `combination` explicitly. `all` means every supplied bound must pass; `any` must have a paper-specific justification.
- Record unit conversion before comparison.
- For multiple seeds, record raw values, minimum runs, aggregation, mean, and standard deviation.
- `equivalence_interval` uses a 95% normal interval in V1.2 and needs at least two observations.
- `non_inferiority` requires a direction and margin.
- Numeric arrays and tables require matching shapes and element-wise success; labeled tables also require matching columns and row labels.
- Rankings and curves may use exact order or Spearman correlation where scientifically appropriate.
- Categorical, string, set, artifact-hash, and distribution claims require their explicit typed comparison methods.
- Visual-only claims remain manual.

## Interpretation boundaries

- Process success is not claim success.
- Metric agreement is not causal proof.
- A failed run is not evidence that the paper is false.
- SHA-256 is byte integrity, not identity or authenticity.
- Live external data must have its URL and response hash recorded; disclose when redistribution terms prevent bundling the raw response.
