# Call for independent planning reviewers

We are seeking research-software engineers and computational researchers to
independently score the three published Codex planning-pilot cases.

Reviewers score claim identification, command mapping, data/license handling,
budget design, and comparison policy from 0 to 2 using
[`evaluations/protocol.md`](../evaluations/protocol.md). Each review must bind to
the exact plan SHA-256, disclose affiliation and conflicts, explain its decision,
and list any proposed modification. Reviews may use a stable public pseudonym;
private contact details should not be committed.

The project will publish the original review, any disagreement, and a separate
maintainer adjudication. It will not relabel self-review as independent review or
claim completion before real reviewer records are accepted.

To volunteer, open a GitHub Issue titled `Independent review: <case-id>` and
state your relevant background, intended case, affiliation, and conflicts. Do not
include restricted paper content, credentials, private datasets, or personal
contact information in the Issue.
