# Pilot result

All three Codex drafts were converted into bounded, preview-first plans and bound to deterministically verified evidence bundles. The most important revisions were scientific scope, predeclared evaluation policy, data/license provenance, and the boundary between metric agreement and broader paper conclusions.

This is evidence that the architecture can publish the interpretation-to-execution handoff. It is not yet evidence of planning accuracy at scale because the sample is three cases, review was not independent, and per-case model/token telemetry was unavailable.

The funded evaluation target is 20–30 public paper/repository pairs with independent human scoring across claim identification, command mapping, data/license handling, budget design, and comparison policy, plus public rejected and blocked cases.
