# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and users must participate in a respectful,
professional, and harassment-free way. Constructive disagreement about evidence,
methods, licenses, safety, or reproducibility is welcome; personal attacks,
discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, and publication of private data or
credentials are not.

## Research and review integrity

- disclose material conflicts when reviewing plans or adjudicating scores;
- do not fabricate runs, identities, adoption, citations, telemetry, or evidence;
- do not upload restricted papers, private datasets, direct identifiers, secrets,
  or confidential repository content;
- distinguish model output, self-review, independent review, deterministic
  verification, and scientific conclusions.

## Enforcement

Report security-sensitive conduct privately through
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Report other conduct concerns to the maintainers
through a GitHub Issue that omits private or identifying information. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, reject reviews, or restrict
participation when necessary to protect people and project integrity.

Enforcement decisions should document the applicable rule, preserve
confidentiality, avoid conflicts of interest, and provide a proportionate
response. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is prohibited.

## Scope

This Code applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in
public or private community interactions.
